Terms and Conditions of Use

Please read these Terms and Conditions carefully before using the Online Library System. By registering an account and signing in, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by the terms set out below.

1. Account Registration

  • You must provide a valid Student ID Number consisting of exactly eight (8) digits. Email addresses, letters, and special characters are not accepted.
  • You must provide your complete Full Name, including both your given name and your surname. Registrations that omit the surname will be rejected.
  • You must provide a contact Phone Number so the library can reach you regarding overdue books or reservations.
  • All three fields (Student ID, Full Name, Phone) are mandatory. None may be left empty.
  • You are responsible for keeping your Student ID confidential. Sharing your Student ID allows others to access your account.

2. Borrowing Limits and Period

  • Each student may hold a maximum of two (2) active borrowings at any time.
  • A student may not borrow a new book while they already have any unreturned book. Because this rule applies to every unreturned book, in practice a student can only have one active borrowing at a time. The maximum of two books is retained as an absolute ceiling.
  • The initial borrowing period is three (3) days, starting from the date the book is borrowed.
  • Each borrowing record may be extended exactly once, for an additional two (2) days. A second extension is not permitted under any circumstances.
  • A book that is currently Borrowed or Reserved cannot be borrowed by another student until it is returned or the reservation is canceled.

3. Reservations

  • You may reserve any book whose status is Available.
  • A reserved book cannot be borrowed or reserved by another student.
  • You may convert one of your active reservations into a borrowing at any time, subject to the borrowing limits in Section 2.
  • You may cancel an active reservation at any time. Canceled reservations are preserved in your transaction history.
  • Reservations that have already been converted into a borrowing (status: Completed) cannot be canceled.

4. Returns

  • You may return a borrowed book at any time before or after its due date.
  • Upon return, the borrowing record is preserved in your transaction history. Returned books are not removed from your records.
  • If the returned book has sustained damage, the appropriate damage penalty (see Section 6) will be applied and recorded against your account.

5. Overdue Penalties

  • Books not returned by their due date are considered overdue.
  • An overdue penalty of PHP 20.00 (₱20) per completed overdue day is charged to your account.
  • For example, a book that is one full day overdue incurs a penalty of ₱20; a book five full days overdue incurs ₱100.
  • Overdue penalties are calculated by the system based on the recorded due date and the current date. Penalties submitted by the browser are never trusted.
  • A student who has not returned an overdue book cannot borrow or reserve another book until the overdue book is returned and the penalty is settled.
  • Penalties are recorded as unpaid until a payment feature is implemented. The system will never claim a penalty has been paid.

6. Damage Penalties

  • Minor damage (e.g. torn page, light stains): ₱50.
  • Moderate damage (e.g. water damage, broken spine): ₱150.
  • Severe damage (e.g. missing pages, unreadable): ₱300.

6.1 Lost Book Penalty

  • If a borrowed book is lost, the borrower is charged a fixed penalty of ₱1,000.
  • The borrower must settle this penalty before they can borrow or reserve another book.

6.2 Expired Reservation Penalty

  • A reservation that is not canceled or converted into a borrowing within seven (7) days automatically expires.
  • An expired reservation incurs a penalty of ₱50.
  • The borrower must settle this penalty before they can borrow or reserve another book.

7. Digital Receipts

  • A digital receipt is generated automatically after every successful borrowing transaction.
  • Each receipt contains a unique receipt number, your Student ID, your Full Name, the book title and author, the borrowing date and time, the due date, the borrowing duration, and the transaction status.
  • Receipts are saved permanently to data/library-data.txt and remain accessible through your profile and transaction history after closing the receipt screen, signing out, or restarting the application.
  • Closing a receipt in the browser does not delete the receipt.

8. Data Persistence and Privacy

  • All account and transaction records are stored locally in data/library-data.txt on the computer running the server.
  • No data is transmitted to any external server. The application is designed for offline use on a single computer.
  • Because authentication relies on Student ID only (no password), this system is intended for educational demonstration and should not be deployed in a production environment without additional security controls.

9. Acceptance

By checking the "I have read and agree to the Terms and Conditions" box during registration, you confirm that you have read these Terms, that you understand them, and that you agree to comply with all of the rules described above. Failure to comply with these rules may result in borrowing privileges being suspended.
